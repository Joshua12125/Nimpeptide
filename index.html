Our Process

From Sourcing to Delivery
Every Step Verified

We continually evaluate our manufacturing partners and quality control processes to maintain the standards our customers expect. Nothing leaves our network without passing full verification.

01
Partner Qualification

Every product is sourced through carefully selected manufacturing partners with documented quality management systems. We evaluate each partner on an ongoing basis to ensure consistent standards.

02
Independent Lab Testing

All products undergo independent third-party analytical testing performed by accredited laboratories. Identity and purity are verified against strict standards before any batch is cleared for sale.

03
Batch Verification

Each batch is individually verified and assigned a unique batch identifier. Results are documented and made available to customers as part of the batch-specific Certificate of Analysis for that lot.

04
COA Issued

A full Certificate of Analysis is compiled from independent laboratory results for each batch. This document is available on our COA page at nimpeptides.com/coas for customer review at any time.

05
Secure Dispatch

Verified batches are securely packaged and dispatched with full order tracking. All orders over $250 ship free. Packaging is designed to be discreet, protective, and maintain product integrity in transit.

Do you offer free shipping?▾
Yes. Free shipping is available on all orders over $250. All shipments are packaged securely and discreetly with full order tracking provided. For orders under $250, standard shipping rates apply based on destination and selected service level.

Every product is sourced through carefully selected manufacturing partners and undergoes independent third-party analytical testing to verify identity and purity before being offered for sale.
